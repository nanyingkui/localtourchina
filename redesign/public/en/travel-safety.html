Travel safety and booking support

Start with LocalTourChina if you need help with a booking or service

Share your booking reference, date, location and what you need help with. We’ll check the order, handle issues with bookings we arrange and help contact the local operator. For immediate danger or urgent medical care, seek local emergency help and alert on-site staff first.

Check who is responsible for your booking before paying

LocalTourChina provides travel information and booking services. Your order identifies the contracting party, payment recipient, local operator and contact for changes or refunds. Plan your own free time. The booking confirmation sets out the services you purchased. Contact us about a missing reservation, incorrect date or name, or duplicate charge. We’ll check and help arrange a correction, refund or other remedy under the applicable terms and law.

If a service goes wrong on the day

For closures, equipment issues or admission disputes, follow site staff instructions and contact us about your booking and next steps. If a vehicle breaks down, is being driven unsafely or is involved in a crash, get to safety before contacting the carrier and LocalTourChina. We check that each guide we arrange holds a valid guide credential. If a guide misses a pickup, gives incorrect directions or omits an agreed service, tell us when and where it happened so we can help the assigning agency address it. Responsibility follows the facts, contract and applicable law.

Changes, cancellations and charges

Rain, fog or poor visibility while an attraction is open generally does not trigger free changes or cancellation. We can discuss changes to the order or length of visits. New dates or slots at Tianmen Mountain, the National Forest Park or the Grand Canyon depend on availability and venue rules. If a closure, safety issue or service failure affects your itinerary, we’ll explain your options and how charges will be settled. We’ll explain any additional charge before asking you to approve a route change. Cancellations and refunds follow your booking terms and applicable law.

Departure, hotel check-in and free time

Before leaving, check the name on your booking, document validity, terminal and station. If you miss a flight or meeting, ask the airline or booking team about availability and costs. If a hotel cannot find the booking, assigns the wrong room or is overbooked, keep your confirmation and contact the hotel and booking team. Before going out on your own, save the hotel’s Chinese address, your return time and your companions’ contact details.

On-site hazards and incident records

Follow staff directions if cable cars or boats stop, trails get slippery, crowds build or access to a glass bridge is restricted. Stop using an unsafe vehicle. For a fall, allergic reaction, collision or lost property, seek help first. Keep booking records, receipts, closure notices and incident records for the attraction, carrier, travel agency or insurer as appropriate.

Travel documents and entry

International travelers should check entry, visa and accommodation registration requirements for their nationality, purpose and length of stay. Chinese travelers should carry valid ID. If you lose a foreign passport, get to safety, report the loss to local police, contact your embassy or consulate and check exit requirements with the local immigration authority. For a lost Chinese ID, ask public security about replacement.

Attractions and outdoor activities

Observe barriers, closures and staff instructions. Move to safety in storms, high winds, floods or poor visibility. Check age, height and health limits for glass bridges, cable cars, boats, hikes and water activities. Contact your booking team about changes after a closure.

Transport and separation

Check the driver, plate and meeting point, and wear a seat belt. Keep children and travelers needing assistance within reach. If separated, stay in a safe public place and contact your group and the on-site contact. Use a verified vehicle.

Health, accommodation and belongings

Choose activities suited to your health. Tell staff about allergies and mobility needs, carry prescribed medicines and locate your hotel’s exits. Keep documents, phones and valuables secure. Verify payment recipients and report theft or fraud to police.

Emergency assistance

In China call 110 for police, 120 for medical emergencies or 119 for fire. Seek local emergency help first, then notify your booking contact and insurer. International travelers can also contact their embassy or consulate in China.

Insurance and responsibility for losses

Arrange travel insurance for the full trip and planned activities before traveling. Take care of your own documents and belongings. Replacement, rebooking or lodging costs arising from your own actions are allocated under the circumstances, contract and applicable law. Other parties remain responsible for their own fault, contractual obligations and duties to assist. This acknowledgment preserves your legal rights. Any consent to process passport photos is requested separately.
