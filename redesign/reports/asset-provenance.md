# Image provenance and optimization

Existing real destination, vehicle, hotel, food, people and legal-document photos remain authentic. Responsive WebP encoding uses the original source; no invented documentary scenery. Four previously black destination images were replaced with genuine existing photos (see dark-image-repairs.json). Legal/brand source documents remain available.

Two new service illustrations were generated using the built-in `image_gen.imagegen` tool. They are illustrations, not photos or representations of an offered vehicle. No third-party generation CLI was used.

## Combined services

Output: `public/assets/designed/combined-services-png-{320,640,960,1440,1536}.webp`.

Prompt: Use case: product-mockup. One production website raster illustration for LocalTourChina's combined travel services. Restrained Apple Store style, studio display on light gray #f5f5f7, cobalt #0071e3 details, white and graphite. A compact elegant arrangement of a small unbranded white touring minibus model, two white admission tickets with blue geometric notches (blank, no writing), a folded abstract itinerary map and a small blue location pin. Clearly a stylized high-end 3D illustration, NOT a real vehicle/product being offered. Soft shadows, neutral clean materials, no glass decorative blobs, no gradients in background, no orange. Landscape 3:2 format; centered with 15% margin, clean silhouette suited to both 140px category tile and 380px service card. No text, no logos, no real people, no watermark. Single unified composition, no collage/UI.
