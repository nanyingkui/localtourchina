Server deployment: inquiry-email Edge Function and new_inquiry_email_outbox migration. Initial settings are disabled and unconfigured. Existing Auth, Kakao connections and customer records are not changed. No historical inquiry backfill is performed. The minute scheduler does nothing while disabled.

Authenticated administrators configure a sending-only Resend key through the admin page. Keys are stored encrypted in Vault and are never returned, logged, written to browser storage, included in email, or placed in tracked source. Recipient is pinned to the Auth-verified administrator email. Sender is limited to localtourchina.com and its subdomains. Status/setup/test/enable validate the bearer identity with Auth and is_admin; the worker uses its own Vault secret. verify_jwt=false is deliberate because the function authenticates both admin requests and the private scheduled worker itself. Foreign browser origins and unauthenticated requests are denied. Mail tables and internal RPCs have no anon/authenticated grants; service-only secret helpers are search_path-pinned.

A separate queue scans committed new website inquiries after activation. Customer submission does not depend on mail delivery. Queue uniqueness prevents duplicate events. Payloads are frozen in the database before provider calls; retries use a stable Resend idempotency key and end within 20 hours, before the documented 24-hour provider dedup retention. Uncertain results are recorded for review. Provider acceptance is displayed as acceptance, never as proven inbox delivery. An additional cap of 90 provider requests per rolling 24 hours includes tests; no paid subscription or automatic upgrade is configured. Test requests are throttled. Confirm receipt before enabling.
