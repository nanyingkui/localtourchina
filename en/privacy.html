Passport photos and travel insurance

We request a passport photo only if an attraction ticket, hotel or vehicle booking requires it. Please keep it out of initial inquiries, general forms and public chats.

Before requesting a photo, we explain what is needed, why, who will receive it and how to submit it. We ask for separate consent and share only the details needed with the relevant booking provider. We use the photo solely for that booking. We keep the photo only as long as necessary for the booking, unless the law requires longer. Use the contact below to request access, correction or deletion.

Please buy your own travel insurance for the full trip before departing for China.

Check that your policy covers China, your full travel dates and planned activities, including medical care, accidents and emergency assistance. LocalTourChina provides travel information and booking help for the services you choose. Your booking confirmation sets out the services included. When insurance is automatically included with an attraction ticket at issuance, coverage follows that ticket and its policy terms. Arrange separate insurance for your full trip.

Share only what is needed to plan your trip.
