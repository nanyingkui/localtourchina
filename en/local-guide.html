Guiding and service hours

One Korean-speaking tour guide for up to 6 hours per day. The standard guide and a request for the owner use the same base rate. Select dates, daily hours, language and party size to see an estimate. A named guide is subject to availability.

Included

On-site guiding at the agreed places and times. Consecutive dates are billed per day. List nonconsecutive dates or differing daily hours in your request for a separate quote.

Exclusions and limits

Vehicles, transport, tickets, meals and accommodation for both guests and the guide are separate. Confirm any arrangements in advance. Interpreting, other languages and groups of 13 or more require a separate service quote.

Beyond 6 hours per day, guests pay the guide ₩20,000 per hour directly, separate from the base guiding fee. Agree partial-hour billing and each day’s finish time before departure.
