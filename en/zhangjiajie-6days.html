Will it suit our group?

What if our flights are at different times?

This page assumes late arrival and a flight around 23:00 on the last day. Earlier arrivals require a review of the Tianmen day, meals and service time. Earlier return flights require a revised cave, canyon and airport sequence. One price does not cover every flight pattern.

Is it suitable for travelling with parents?

Tell us how long they usually walk and how they manage steps. Even with cable cars, viewpoints, caves and canyons involve walking. We’ll check feasible sections and rest stops for the group’s fitness.

What if we’re afraid of heights?

Tell us before booking if Tianmen’s cliff paths or the glass bridge concern you. Before tickets are chosen, we’ll explain possible routes and any price changes for omissions or alternatives.
