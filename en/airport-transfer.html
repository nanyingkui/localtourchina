Arriving and leaving
need different plans.

For pickup, we plan where to meet after arrivals. For drop-off, we work back from when you need to reach the airport. Your exact hotel and flight help us agree on the meeting point and departure time.

  1. 01

    First, your flight and hotel

    Tell us the date, flight number, hotel, group size and number of suitcases. The route and fare depend on whether your hotel is downtown or in Wulingyuan.

  2. 02

    Meet at the agreed airport location

    Check the driver’s contact details and meeting point provided after booking. If immigration or baggage collection takes longer, let us know so we can adjust the wait and transfer.

  3. 03

    For your flight home, plan backwards

    We allow for the drive from your hotel and your airline’s check-in requirements. A transfer with sightseeing stops needs a separate arrangement.

An easier arrival starts outside the airport.

Start with your group and luggage.

A nine-seat vehicle carries up to eight guests; a fourteen-seat vehicle up to thirteen. Large suitcases and strollers need a luggage check even when seats are available. Send your flight, exact hotel branch, and suitcase sizes and count.

What the service covers.

What we arrange,
what we check separately.

Included
Private vehicle and driver for the agreed route, road and parking fees, night operation and waiting within the itinerary.
Not included
Guide, admission tickets, travellers’ meals and accommodation. Extra stops, extended hours, unusual luggage and child seats need advance confirmation.
Vehicle and luggage
A 9-seat vehicle takes up to 8 travellers; a 14-seat vehicle takes up to 13. Seat counts include the driver. We check luggage space and the right vehicle for your suitcases.
Meeting up and changes
Driver contact, meeting point and vehicle details are provided after booking. Flight changes or extra stops may affect the arrangements and price. This service does not guarantee a Korean-speaking driver.

Before you book.

Can I use this for a late-night arrival?

Night operation is included in the quoted vehicle fare for the agreed route. We first confirm your flight and driver availability. If your flight changes, we explain any revised waiting and transfer conditions.

Does a Korean-speaking guide come too?

This is a vehicle-and-driver service. If you need a Korean-speaking guide, check the separate guide service or a trip that includes one.
