If your holiday is short,
start with the views you came for.

Even with a late arrival,
three full days to explore.

Rest at the hotel on day one, visit Tianmen on day two, the forest park on day three, and Huanglong Cave and the Grand Canyon on your last day. A short trip with different ways to experience Zhangjiajie’s signature scenery.

Three sightseeing days

Town & Tianmen / Forest park / Cave & canyon

Three nights in one place

3 hotel nights / 9 meals / 1 show and 1 massage

There is walking through the final day. Based on late arrival and a flight home around 23:00. Huangshizhai, Golden Whip Stream and Furong are not included.

Arrival night

Once you land,
leave the wayfinding to us.

After leaving arrivals, meet your Korean-speaking guide at the location provided in advance. Load your suitcases and head to the hotel. No sightseeing is planned on this late-arrival day.

After check-in

Pack just a small bag for tomorrow.

Check the breakfast location and next morning’s meeting time. The guide arranges a vehicle for your flight and bags; room and bed setup are explained before booking.

Arrival-day meals are not included in the base rate. We’ll check what is available for your arrival time.

From an ordinary morning
to a doorway in the sky.

Downtown supermarket & coffee time → lunch → Tianmen → dinner

From the everyday to the scale of the mountains.

Morning · start in town

Choose unfamiliar flavours with a little help.

Show the guide a packet if you’re curious about the flavour. Ask whether it’s spicy or good for sharing, then choose what appeals. During coffee time, sit down, rest and hear about the afternoon. Drinks and snacks are extra.

Even a relaxed morning has a plan.We arrange the morning around Tianmen’s entry time.

Shop opening times and your Tianmen booking determine the order. The itinerary does not guarantee a visit to the exact shop in the photo.

Afternoon · Tianmen Mountain

The size you saw in photos,
now right in front of you.

Look up into the opening carved through the rock at Tianmen Cave. On the summit paths, stay inside the railings and pause where the views open out. Tell your guide the group photo you’d like.

After descending · dinner and rest

After the first mountain,
sit down and unwind.

One 80-minute foot-and-body massage is included. We first consider a slot after descending, then arrange a suitable day around Tianmen queues and actual reservations.

We’ll prepare ahead.We check the ways up and down too.

Transport and escalators on the booked route reduce the stair climbing. Tell us before booking if heights or glass floors concern you.

Meals & stayBreakfast, lunch and dinner included
Stay in the same downtown hotel

Walking & transportThere is still walking and stair climbing on Tianmen’s summit. Admission, booked-route transport, escalators and shoe covers included.

Ten-Mile Gallery → Tianzi up → Yuanjiajie → Bailong down → Charming Xiangxi

Up by cable car. Down by Bailong Elevator.

Morning · Ten-Mile Gallery and Tianzi

Look up from the little train,
then down from the cable car.

Pick out the rock shapes from the Ten-Mile Gallery train. On the Tianzi cable car, the trees you just saw drop below your feet. Walk together between viewpoints and pause where the view opens.

Midday · mountain lunch and Yuanjiajie

After the photograph,
look a little longer.

Have lunch and a seated rest on the mountain, then continue to Yuanjiajie. Alternate between one close pillar and the mountains behind it. Hand your phone to the guide so the usual photographer can join the group.

Why we rest on the mountain.No trip down just for lunch.

We plan park-shuttle rides and transfers between viewing areas together. Even on a private trip, park transport is shared with other visitors and may involve queues.

To finish the day,

One booked Charming Xiangxi performance after dinner. This drawing explains the visit; it is not a photograph of the show.

Evening · Charming Xiangxi

Rock shapes by day.
Sound and movement by night.

After descending and having dinner, head to the theatre. From your seat, take in the music and movement, then compare favourite moments afterwards. Actual performance times and seating conditions are provided with your booking.

Through a mountain,

Check out → Huanglong Cave → lunch → Grand Canyon → dinner → airport

One more scene on the way home.

Morning · Huanglong Cave

Up close, the texture of the rock.
Look up, and the scale of the cave.

Leave checked-out luggage in the vehicle and take a small bag inside. Notice the rock’s surface, then follow its shapes towards the ceiling. Walk the steps and paths at your group’s pace.

We’ll prepare ahead.No need to return to the hotel for your bags.

We prepare the vehicle and luggage so you can head straight to the airport after sightseeing. The cave boat ride is checked against your actual ticket and on-site operations.

Afternoon · Grand Canyon

Take the first step slowly.
Look wherever feels comfortable.

Look through the glass or towards the mountains—whichever feels better. Confirm the way across with your guide and walk together. Take photos where you won’t block others.

Work backwards from your flight.
  1. Confirm the flight and target airport arrival time
  2. Allow for the canyon exit, drive and dinner
  3. Choose walking sections and rest stops within that time

still a sightseeing day.

This plan assumes a flight around 23:00. The cave and canyon have steps and walking. For an earlier flight, we reduce the final day’s scope rather than cutting airport check-in time to fit more sightseeing.

Will it suit our group?

What if our flights are at different times?

This page assumes late arrival and a flight around 23:00 on the last day. Earlier arrivals require a review of the Tianmen day, meals and service time. Earlier return flights require a revised cave, canyon and airport sequence. One price does not cover every flight pattern.

Is it suitable for travelling with parents?

Tell us how long they usually walk and how they manage steps. Even with cable cars, viewpoints, caves and canyons involve walking. We’ll check feasible sections and rest stops for the group’s fitness.

What if we’re afraid of heights?

Tell us before booking if Tianmen’s cliff paths or the glass bridge concern you. Before tickets are chosen, we’ll explain possible routes and any price changes for omissions or alternatives.
