Where we start, and where we return.

The same sights,
but a different drive.

Downtown and Wulingyuan have different start and finish points. We check your day’s route before calculating the total vehicle fare.

  1. 01

    Forest park, then the show

    Confirm the hotel–forest park–Charming Xiangxi–hotel route. The drive after the show is included; park and show tickets and a guide are separate.

  2. 02

    A day around Tianmen and town

    We arrange downtown stops and hotel transfers around your Tianmen entry time. Tell us in advance about any extra places or longer journeys.

  3. 03

    A day linking the cave and canyon

    Travel between Huanglong Cave and the Grand Canyon, finishing in your hotel area. Continuing to the airport is different from a standard return journey and needs a revised quote.

What the service covers.

What we arrange,
what we check separately.

Included
Private vehicle and driver for the agreed route, road and parking fees, night operation and waiting within the itinerary.
Not included
Guide, admission tickets, travellers’ meals and accommodation. Extra stops, extended hours, unusual luggage and child seats need advance confirmation.
Vehicle and luggage
A 9-seat vehicle takes up to 8 travellers; a 14-seat vehicle takes up to 13. Seat counts include the driver. We check luggage space and the right vehicle for your suitcases.
Meeting up and changes
Driver contact, meeting point and vehicle details are provided after booking. Flight changes or extra stops may affect the arrangements and price. This service does not guarantee a Korean-speaking driver.

The vehicle links the agreed stops outside the park. Park shuttles, cable cars, elevators and admission tickets are separate and subject to local transport operations.
