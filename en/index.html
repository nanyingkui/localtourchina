Guide services and assignment

How we approach travel

Your trip. Your choices.

We guide carefully, explain first and decide together.

  1. No pressure to buy or add activities

    Shopping and extra visits are optional. Declining a purchase or an extra does not change the service we have agreed to provide.

  2. Extra costs, explained before you choose

    For an additional stop, more guide time or another change, we check what is possible and the cost before you decide.

  3. Walking and food needs come first

    Tell us about stairs, rest breaks and ingredients you find difficult. We discuss practical alternatives within the agreed itinerary.

  4. Contact us if something goes wrong

    If the guiding or attitude makes you uncomfortable, contact LocalTourChina through the enquiry channel. We will understand the situation and discuss workable next steps.

Weather, attraction operations and booking changes remain subject to actual conditions and the service provider’s terms.

Before you make your decision

Know the plan. Understand the price.

We discuss your group’s needs first, then put the important booking details in writing.

  1. Start with what you know

    Dates can still be undecided. Tell us your group size, the scenery you want to see and your walking preferences.

  2. Review the itinerary and quote

    Check the hotel and room, inclusions and exclusions, per-person price and group total for your actual requirements.

  3. Decide after checking the terms

    Confirm who provides and collects payment for the services, deposits and balances, and change and cancellation conditions.
