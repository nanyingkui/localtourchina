What if my parents tire while walking?

We check walking, steps and rest stops beforehand. On site, we coordinate breaks, a return route and the pickup point. Extra transport costs are checked first.

What if the food is too spicy or unfamiliar?

Tell us about spice, ingredients, texture and meal times. We help check orders and discuss alternatives. Allergies require a separate check of preparation and possible cross-contact with the restaurant.

What happens when fog or rain changes the plan?

We check weather and actual operations, then discuss feasible visit orders and alternatives. Changes, cancellations and extra costs depend on provider rules and availability.
