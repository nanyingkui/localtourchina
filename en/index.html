4 days · Tianmen Mountain and the Forest Park

Zhangjiajie Essentials · 4 days, 3 nights

View details ↗
Itinerary, inclusions & exclusions

Day by day

Early arrival · afternoon departure

  1. Early arrival and Tianmen Mountain

    Reference: meet at 10:00 → luggage arrangements and lunch at 11:00 → Tianmen Mountain at the reserved entry time. Follow the booked route using operating cableways, shuttles and escalators, Tianmen Cave and open summit paths. Hotel check-in around 17:30, then dinner. Flight delays or entry slots may require a different order.

  2. Forest Park: Yuanjiajie and Tianzi Mountain

    Meet at the hotel around 08:00 → East Gate → Ten-Mile Gallery train → Tianzi cableway and viewpoints → simple mountain lunch → Yuanjiajie viewpoints → descend by Bailong Elevator. Dinner and rest around 17:30. One Charming Xiangxi performance at the confirmed show time. Pickup accounts for city-hotel travel time and queues.

  3. Huanglong Cave and Grand Canyon

    Leave the hotel around 08:30 → Huanglong Cave and its boat ride → lunch around 11:30 → Grand Canyon glass bridge, glass elevator, walkway and canyon trail. Regroup around 17:00 for the hotel transfer. Cave steps can be slippery and some paths are exposed; adapt to mobility and operating conditions.

  4. Checkout and airport transfer

    Breakfast and checkout → private airport transfer timed to your flight. Departure-day lunch follows the selected plan and actual departure time; no extra hotel night is added.

Late arrival · late-night departure

  1. Arrival and check-in

    Meet at airport arrivals at your actual flight time → private transfer to your city hotel → check in and rest. No sightseeing on a late-arrival day; confirm any arrival-night meal separately.

  2. Local streets and Tianmen Mountain

    Meet around 08:40 → nearby market or neighbourhood walk and a coffee break (drinks at your expense) → lunch around 11:30 → Tianmen Mountain at your reserved entry time, including Tianmen Cave and open summit paths → hotel and dinner. Market opening and entry slots may change the order.

  3. Forest Park: Yuanjiajie and Tianzi Mountain

    Meet at the hotel around 08:00 → East Gate → Ten-Mile Gallery train → Tianzi cableway and viewpoints → simple mountain lunch → Yuanjiajie viewpoints → descend by Bailong Elevator. Dinner and rest around 17:30. One Charming Xiangxi performance at the confirmed show time. Pickup accounts for city-hotel travel time and queues.

  4. Cave and canyon, then the airport

    Check out around 08:00 with luggage in the vehicle → Huanglong Cave → lunch → Grand Canyon → early dinner → directly to the airport. This reference plan assumes a flight around 23:00; work backward from the airline’s check-in requirements with a traffic buffer. Sightseeing must not compromise check-in.
