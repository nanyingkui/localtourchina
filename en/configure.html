05Our promise

We only offer journeys where our local team can look after you in person.

When a flight is delayed or plans suddenly change, even knowing where to go next can feel overwhelming. That’s when the LocalTourChina team steps in to take care of what comes next.

We do not make shopping-store stops or offer paid optional excursions. Trip preparation and guide compensation are included in the trip price from the start. Any separate expenses are explained before departure.
