Travel directly between Changsha and Zhangjiajie with your luggage.

ItineraryInclusions & extrasYour quote

How it works

Choose date, passengers, vehicle and transfer areas for a quote. Confirm hotels, flight or train details, luggage and meeting point with our team before dispatch.

Route & timing

One-way travel in either direction between Changsha Station, Changsha South Station or Huanghua Airport and Zhangjiajie city or Wulingyuan. Changsha round trips are not currently quoted.

Included

  • Vehicle and driver on the confirmed route
  • tolls
  • parking
  • night service and waiting within the agreed itinerary.

Excluded

  • Guiding
  • attraction tickets
  • guest meals and guest accommodation are excluded.
  • Additional stops
  • extensions
  • special luggage and child seats require separate confirmation.

Service boundaries

9 seats include the driver, leaving up to 8 passengers; 14 seats leave up to 13. Larger parties are priced with multiple vehicles. Luggage capacity must be checked.

Pricing

The quote uses the whole-route vehicle fare; a return is not simply twice a one-way fare. This is an estimate; changed conditions require a new quote and staff confirmation.
