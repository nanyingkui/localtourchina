Day tour · Yuanjiajie, Tianzi Mountain and evening show

Wulingyuan National Forest Park

Trip highlights

See Yuanjiajie and Tianzi Mountain in one day if you’re short on time.

How we plan it

We check how the Ten-Mile Gallery, Bailong Elevator, park shuttles and Tianzi cableway connect.

If plans change

If queues build or a lift closes, ask the on-site contact listed in your booking about the descent and onward transfer.

ItineraryInclusions & extrasYour quote

Itinerary

Stop 1

09:00 hotel pickup and forest park entry.

Stop 2

Visit Ten-Mile Gallery and take the Bailong Elevator to Yuanjiajie.

Stop 3

After lunch, transfer via Yangjiajie and visit Tianzi Mountain by cableway.

Stop 4

Free time from 16:30, dinner at your own expense, show at 19:20 and hotel drop-off after the performance, with return time depending on the hotel.

What’s included

Park admission, one cableway ride and one Bailong Elevator ride, Ten-Mile Gallery train, reserved show seat, lunch, Korean-speaking guide and hotel transfers.

Exclusions and booking limits

Confirm any item outside the written quote separately. A reference price or saved selection does not confirm a booking.

Route and booking conditions

Dinner is extra. Show and mountain transport depend on operations and availability.

Private and shared departures

Private departures start with 2 guests; parties of 2–3 can also request a shared departure. A shared tour is confirmed after at least 4 paying guests on the same date and route, transport, guide and tickets are secured. Our team verifies the final group size and confirms the price and savings in writing. If no group forms, discuss another date or a private quote.

Guide service hours

The guiding fee covers this day tour’s full published itinerary. The forest evening show or late Furong return does not incur a guide overtime fee. Additional requests outside the itinerary require separate agreement.

Vehicles & transfers

Choose pickup and drop-off areas separately, including a change of hotel between the city and Wulingyuan. Driver and guide seats are allowed for in 9- and 14-seat vehicles; luggage capacity needs confirmation. Tolls, parking, night service and waiting for the listed itinerary are included.
