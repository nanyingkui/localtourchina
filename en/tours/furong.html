Itinerary

Stop 1

Transfer to Zhangjiajie West for an afternoon train to Furong. Explore the old lanes and waterfall viewpoints.

Stop 2

After dinner, see the illuminated waterfall, take the train back and transfer to your hotel.

What’s included

Second-class return train tickets, local transfers, Korean-speaking guide, admission and dinner.

Exclusions and booking limits

Confirm any item outside the written quote separately. A reference price or saved selection does not confirm a booking.

Route and booking conditions

Afternoon departure and late return. Rail seats and the last train must be checked.

Private and shared departures

Private departures start with 2 guests; parties of 2–3 can also request a shared departure. A shared tour is confirmed after at least 4 paying guests on the same date and route, transport, guide and tickets are secured. Our team verifies the final group size and confirms the price and savings in writing. If no group forms, discuss another date or a private quote.

Guide service hours

The guiding fee covers this day tour’s full published itinerary. The forest evening show or late Furong return does not incur a guide overtime fee. Additional requests outside the itinerary require separate agreement.
