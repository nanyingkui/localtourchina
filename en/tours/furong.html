Furong Town Evening Tour

Trip highlights

Keep your Zhangjiajie hotel and make an evening trip to see Furong lit up.

How we plan it

We coordinate the round-trip trains, station transfers, dinner and time at the waterfall after dark.

If plans change

If train times change, ask your guide how dinner and the evening visit can be adjusted.

ItineraryInclusions & extrasYour quote

Itinerary

Stop 1

Transfer to Zhangjiajie West for an afternoon train to Furong. Explore the old lanes and waterfall viewpoints.

Stop 2

After dinner, see the illuminated waterfall, take the train back and transfer to your hotel.

What’s included

Second-class return train tickets, local transfers, Korean-speaking guide, admission and dinner.

Exclusions and booking limits

Confirm any item outside the written quote separately. A reference price or saved selection does not confirm a booking.

Route and booking conditions

Afternoon departure and late return. Rail seats and the last train must be checked.

Private and shared departures

Private departures start with 2 guests; parties of 2–3 can also request a shared departure. A shared tour is confirmed after at least 4 paying guests on the same date and route, transport, guide and tickets are secured. Our team verifies the final group size and confirms the price and savings in writing. If no group forms, discuss another date or a private quote.

Guide service hours

The guiding fee covers this day tour’s full published itinerary. The forest evening show or late Furong return does not incur a guide overtime fee. Additional requests outside the itinerary require separate agreement.

Booking responsibilities and changes

Before paying, check your order for the contracting party, payment recipient, local operator and refund contact. These depend on the services you book. Ask your booking contact to confirm any missing details.

The contracting party confirms the terms and services. The payment recipient provides payment instructions and a receipt. The local operator handles arrangements on site. The refund contact handles requests and explains the calculation.

If you cancel or skip part of a confirmed booking, any refund is calculated under the booking terms and applicable law. We itemize necessary costs that can lawfully be deducted and return any balance due.

Rain, fog or poor visibility while attractions remain open generally does not qualify for free cancellation or rerouting. We can discuss changing the order or details on the day. Changing dates or time slots for Tianmen Mountain, the National Forest Park or the Grand Canyon depends on availability and venue rules.

If a closure or safety issue prevents the agreed itinerary from going ahead, we coordinate alternatives, rescheduling or settlement under the booking terms and applicable law. Any balance due is refunded accordingly.

If you request a new route or replacement for a confirmed service, we check availability and explain any extra costs and recoverable amounts before asking you to approve the change.

Passport photos and travel insurance

Please buy your own travel insurance for the full trip before departing for China.

Check that your policy covers China, your full travel dates and planned activities, including medical care, accidents and emergency assistance. LocalTourChina provides travel information and booking help for the services you choose. Your booking confirmation sets out the services included. When insurance is automatically included with an attraction ticket at issuance, coverage follows that ticket and its policy terms. Arrange separate insurance for your full trip.
