Day by day

  1. Day 1
    Midday arrival · afternoon Tianmen subject to confirmation

    Arrive around noon, China local time, and meet your driver at the actual arrival time → luggage arrangements and lunch. Visit Tianmen Mountain that afternoon only after confirming that arrival formalities, transfers, queues and the entry slot leave enough time. Follow the booked route and operating conditions, then check in and have dinner. If arrival is later or entry cannot be arranged, rest at the hotel and move sightseeing to a later day. No morning sightseeing or fixed entry and finish times are assumed.

  2. Day 2
    Forest Park: Yuanjiajie and Tianzi Mountain

    Meet at the hotel around 08:00 → East Gate → Ten-Mile Gallery train → Tianzi cableway and viewpoints → simple mountain lunch → Yuanjiajie viewpoints → descend by Bailong Elevator. Dinner and rest around 17:30. One Charming Xiangxi performance at the confirmed show time. Pickup accounts for city-hotel travel time and queues.

  3. Day 3
    Huanglong Cave and Grand Canyon

    Leave the hotel around 08:30 → Huanglong Cave and its boat ride → lunch around 11:30 → Grand Canyon glass bridge, glass elevator, walkway and canyon trail. Regroup around 17:00 for the hotel transfer. Cave steps can be slippery and some paths are exposed; adapt to mobility and operating conditions.

  4. Day 4
    City walk and rest

    After breakfast, an easy local walk and lunch → hotel rest or your included 80-minute massage → dinner. No extra paid attraction is silently added; agree the rest schedule with your party.

  5. Day 5
    Private drive to Fenghuang and overnight

    Check out around 09:00 → your group’s vehicle to Fenghuang → lunch → old-town lanes and riverside walk → hotel check-in → dinner and Tuo River night cruise. Stay in Fenghuang for the final night; no train or station transfers are inserted.

  6. Day 6
    Fenghuang checkout, directly to the airport

    After breakfast, check out at the time calculated from your flight → drive directly to Zhangjiajie Airport. Allow roughly four hours driving plus traffic and check-in buffers. No unnecessary final Zhangjiajie hotel night; early flights require reordering the Fenghuang stay first.

Inclusions

5 nights in city boutique hotels with breakfast; twin sharing, with an additional room included for an unpaired guest. Equivalent hotels are subject to availability. The last night is in an equivalent Fenghuang hotel. The group vehicle and Tuo River night cruise are included; trains and the old-town nine-sight pass are not.

Forest Park, Tianmen and the attractions and internal transport listed in the selected plan, plus one Charming Xiangxi performance

Exclusions and separate charges

Flights and external long-distance transport, visas and insurance, personal expenses, extra meals or drinks, room or hotel upgrades and separately requested attractions. Multi-day guiding is based on about 10 hours per day. Agree longer days beforehand; guests pay the guide ₩20,000 per extra hour directly, including long days with the scheduled show. Agree partial-hour billing before departure.

Itinerary and pricing conditions

All times are reference times in China. Confirm the final order against tickets, weather, mobility and flights. Prices assume the stated city hotel, meals and transport; holidays, Wulingyuan stays or different pickup/drop-off times require a revised quote. Private departures start with two guests.

Booking responsibilities and changes

Before paying, check your order for the contracting party, payment recipient, local operator and refund contact. These depend on the services you book. Ask your booking contact to confirm any missing details.

The contracting party confirms the terms and services. The payment recipient provides payment instructions and a receipt. The local operator handles arrangements on site. The refund contact handles requests and explains the calculation.

If you cancel or skip part of a confirmed booking, any refund is calculated under the booking terms and applicable law. We itemize necessary costs that can lawfully be deducted and return any balance due.

Rain, fog or poor visibility while attractions remain open generally does not qualify for free cancellation or rerouting. We can discuss changing the order or details on the day. Changing dates or time slots for Tianmen Mountain, the National Forest Park or the Grand Canyon depends on availability and venue rules.

If a closure or safety issue prevents the agreed itinerary from going ahead, we coordinate alternatives, rescheduling or settlement under the booking terms and applicable law. Any balance due is refunded accordingly.

If you request a new route or replacement for a confirmed service, we check availability and explain any extra costs and recoverable amounts before asking you to approve the change.

Passport photos and travel insurance

Please buy your own travel insurance for the full trip before departing for China.

Check that your policy covers China, your full travel dates and planned activities, including medical care, accidents and emergency assistance. When insurance is automatically included with an attraction ticket at issuance, coverage follows that ticket and its policy terms. Arrange separate insurance for your full trip.
