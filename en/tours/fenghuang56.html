Day by day

  1. Day 1
    Early arrival and Tianmen Mountain

    Reference: meet at 10:00 → luggage arrangements and lunch at 11:00 → Tianmen Mountain at the reserved entry time. Follow the booked route using operating cableways, shuttles and escalators, Tianmen Cave and open summit paths. Hotel check-in around 17:30, then dinner. Flight delays or entry slots may require a different order.

  2. Day 2
    Forest Park: Yuanjiajie and Tianzi Mountain

    Meet at the hotel around 08:00 → East Gate → Ten-Mile Gallery train → Tianzi cableway and viewpoints → simple mountain lunch → Yuanjiajie viewpoints → descend by Bailong Elevator. Dinner and rest around 17:30. One Charming Xiangxi performance at the confirmed show time. Pickup accounts for city-hotel travel time and queues.

  3. Day 3
    Huanglong Cave and Grand Canyon

    Leave the hotel around 08:30 → Huanglong Cave and its boat ride → lunch around 11:30 → Grand Canyon glass bridge, glass elevator, walkway and canyon trail. Regroup around 17:00 for the hotel transfer. Cave steps can be slippery and some paths are exposed; adapt to mobility and operating conditions.

  4. Day 4
    City walk and rest

    After breakfast, an easy local walk and lunch → hotel rest or your included 80-minute massage → dinner. No extra paid attraction is silently added; agree the rest schedule with your party.

  5. Day 5
    Private drive to Fenghuang and overnight

    Check out around 09:00 → your group’s vehicle to Fenghuang → lunch → old-town lanes and riverside walk → hotel check-in → dinner and Tuo River night cruise. Stay in Fenghuang for the final night; no train or station transfers are inserted.

  6. Day 6
    Fenghuang checkout, directly to the airport

    After breakfast, check out at the time calculated from your flight → drive directly to Zhangjiajie Airport. Allow roughly four hours driving plus traffic and check-in buffers. No unnecessary final Zhangjiajie hotel night; early flights require reordering the Fenghuang stay first.

Inclusions

5 nights in city boutique hotels with breakfast; twin sharing, with an additional room included for an unpaired guest. Korean-speaking guide, adequate vehicle seats for guests and guides, driver, parking and road tolls, Zhangjiajie airport transfers, the meals in the selected plan and one 80-minute massage. Equivalent hotels are subject to availability. The last night is in an equivalent Fenghuang hotel. The group vehicle and Tuo River night cruise are included; trains and the old-town nine-sight pass are not.

Forest Park, Tianmen and the attractions and internal transport listed in the selected plan, plus one Charming Xiangxi performance

Exclusions and separate charges

Flights and external long-distance transport, visas and insurance, personal expenses, extra meals or drinks, room or hotel upgrades and separately requested attractions. Multi-day guiding is based on about 10 hours per day. Agree longer days beforehand; guests pay the guide ₩20,000 per extra hour directly, including long days with the scheduled show. Agree partial-hour billing before departure.

Itinerary and pricing conditions

All times are reference times in China. Confirm the final order against tickets, weather, mobility and flights. Prices assume the stated city hotel, meals and transport; holidays, Wulingyuan stays or different pickup/drop-off times require a revised quote. Private departures start with two guests.
