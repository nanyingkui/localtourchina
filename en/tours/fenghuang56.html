Day by day

  1. Day 1
    Midday arrival · afternoon Tianmen subject to confirmation

    Airport meeting around noon → luggage and lunch

    Tianmen and open summit paths if an afternoon entry is confirmed → hotel and dinner

    For this day

    Same-day sightseeing depends on arrival, transfer and queue times and the entry slot. If these do not fit, rest at the hotel and reschedule the visit.

    Arrival-day details

    Arrive around noon, China local time, and meet your driver at the actual arrival time → luggage arrangements and lunch. Visit Tianmen Mountain that afternoon only after confirming that arrival formalities, transfers, queues and the entry slot leave enough time. Follow the booked route and operating conditions, then check in and have dinner. If arrival is later or entry cannot be arranged, rest at the hotel and move sightseeing to a later day. No morning sightseeing or fixed entry and finish times are assumed.

  2. Day 2
    Forest Park: Yuanjiajie and Tianzi Mountain

    Meet at the hotel around 08:00 → East Gate → Ten-Mile Gallery train

    Tianzi cableway and viewpoints → simple mountain lunch → Yuanjiajie viewpoints

    descend by Bailong Elevator. → Dinner and rest around 17:30. → One Charming Xiangxi performance at the confirmed show time.

    For this day

    Pickup accounts for city-hotel travel time and queues.

  3. Day 3
    Huanglong Cave and Grand Canyon

    Leave the hotel around 08:30 → Huanglong Cave and its boat ride

    lunch around 11:30 → Grand Canyon glass bridge, glass elevator, walkway and canyon trail.

    Regroup around 17:00 for the hotel transfer.

    For this day

    Cave steps can be slippery and some paths are exposed; adapt to mobility and operating conditions.

  4. Day 4
    City walk and rest

    After breakfast, an easy local walk and lunch → hotel rest or your included 80-minute massage

    dinner.

    For this day

    No extra paid attraction is silently added; agree the rest schedule with your party.

  5. Day 5
    Private drive to Fenghuang and overnight

    Check out around 09:00 → your group’s vehicle to Fenghuang

    lunch → old-town lanes and riverside walk

    hotel check-in → dinner and Tuo River night cruise.

    For this day

    Stay in Fenghuang for the final night; no train or station transfers are inserted.

  6. Day 6
    Fenghuang checkout, directly to the airport

    After breakfast, check out at the time calculated from your flight → drive directly to Zhangjiajie Airport.

    For this day

    Allow roughly four hours driving plus traffic and check-in buffers.

    No unnecessary final Zhangjiajie hotel night; early flights require reordering the Fenghuang stay first.

Guide overtime

Guiding is based on about 10 hours a day. Agree extra hours in advance and pay the guide ₩20,000 per extra hour directly. Confirm hours for longer days with shows, and partial-hour billing, before departure.

Itinerary and pricing conditions

All times are reference times in China. Confirm the final order against tickets, weather, mobility and flights. Prices assume the stated city hotel, meals and transport; holidays, Wulingyuan stays or different pickup/drop-off times require a revised quote. Private departures start with two guests.
