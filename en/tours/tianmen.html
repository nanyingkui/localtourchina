Day tour · Local market and Tianmen Mountain

Tianmen Mountain

Trip highlights

Spend the morning at the market and in town, then head to Tianmen Mountain in the afternoon.

How we plan it

We check cloud cover, visibility, your entry slot and cableway operations before the ascent.

If plans change

If your slot or the cableway changes, ask the on-site contact listed in your booking which routes are available.

ItineraryInclusions & extrasYour quote

Itinerary

Stop 1

Meet at 08:40 for the traditional market, temple and coffee.

Stop 2

Lunch at 11:30. Enter Tianmen Mountain at 13:00 for the cableway, glass walkway, Guigu Cliff Path and Tianmen Cave.

Stop 3

Return to central Zhangjiajie after the visit; confirm timing against entry slots and queues.

What’s included

  • Mountain admission
  • round-trip cableway and escalators
  • glass walkway
  • coffee
  • lunch
  • local guide (confirm your preferred language and the applicable quote) and city transport.

Exclusions and booking limits

  • Confirm any item outside the written quote separately.
  • A reference price or saved selection does not confirm a booking.

Route and booking conditions

Cableways and cliff paths are subject to day-of operations.

Private and shared departures

Private departures start with 2 guests; parties of 2–3 can also request a shared departure. A shared tour is confirmed after at least 4 paying guests on the same date and route, transport, guide and tickets are secured. Our team verifies the final group size and confirms the price and savings in writing. If no group forms, discuss another date or a private quote.

Guide service hours

The guiding fee covers this day tour’s full published itinerary. The forest evening show or late Furong return does not incur a guide overtime fee. Additional requests outside the itinerary require separate agreement.

Vehicles & transfers

Choose pickup and drop-off areas separately, including a change of hotel between the city and Wulingyuan. Driver and guide seats are allowed for in 9- and 14-seat vehicles; luggage capacity needs confirmation. Tolls, parking, night service and waiting for the listed itinerary are included.
