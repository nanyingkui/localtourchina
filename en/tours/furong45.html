Zhangjiajie & Furong · 5 days, 4 nights

Day by day

  1. Day 1
    Early arrival and Tianmen Mountain

    Reference: meet at 10:00 → luggage arrangements and lunch at 11:00 → Tianmen Mountain at the reserved entry time. Follow the booked route using operating cableways, shuttles and escalators, Tianmen Cave and open summit paths. Hotel check-in around 17:30, then dinner. Flight delays or entry slots may require a different order.

  2. Day 2
    Forest Park: Yuanjiajie and Tianzi Mountain

    Meet at the hotel around 08:00 → East Gate → Ten-Mile Gallery train → Tianzi cableway and viewpoints → simple mountain lunch → Yuanjiajie viewpoints → descend by Bailong Elevator. Dinner and rest around 17:30. One Charming Xiangxi performance at the confirmed show time. Pickup accounts for city-hotel travel time and queues.

  3. Day 3
    Huanglong Cave and Grand Canyon

    Leave the hotel around 08:30 → Huanglong Cave and its boat ride → lunch around 11:30 → Grand Canyon glass bridge, glass elevator, walkway and canyon trail. Regroup around 17:00 for the hotel transfer. Cave steps can be slippery and some paths are exposed; adapt to mobility and operating conditions.

  4. Day 4
    Afternoon Furong Town and waterfall lights

    Rest at the hotel in the morning → lunch → leave around 14:00 in your group’s vehicle for Furong Town → lanes and waterfall viewpoints → dinner → illuminated waterfall and town → return to Zhangjiajie. Avoid an early strenuous hike immediately after the late return.

  5. Day 5
    Checkout and airport transfer

    Breakfast and checkout → private airport transfer timed to your flight. Departure-day lunch follows the selected plan and actual departure time; no extra hotel night is added.
