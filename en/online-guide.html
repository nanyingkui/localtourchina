How the service works

Share your dates, cities and support needs. Confirm availability and the fee before booking. Saving a selection does not confirm a booking.

Included scope

  • One group, one trip, up to 7 days
  • One pre-trip itinerary review and one further review after a major change
  • Online guidance on routes, addresses, transport, restaurants, payment methods and itinerary changes, 08:00–20:00 China time
  • Where text cannot resolve the question, one voice or video call per day, up to 10 minutes

Exclusions and boundaries

No in-person accompaniment, ticket or vehicle costs, booking or purchase agency, advance payments, ongoing third-party liaison, 24-hour standby, emergency rescue or compensation. Please communicate through one group representative. Response times depend on local conditions and connectivity. Trips over 7 days or additional cities and support require a new quote.
