Standard service
One Korean-speaking sightseeing guide: $164.81 for a 6-hour day. Calculated for consecutive days with a group of 1–12.
Beyond 6 hours
Pay $14.98 per additional hour directly to the guide. Confirm how partial hours are charged before booking.
Costs to arrange separately
Transport, tickets, meals and accommodation for both travellers and guide. Vehicles are booked separately.
Guide requests and other arrangements
The base fee is the same for a general guide and a request for the LocalTourChina operator. Assignment depends on availability. Groups of 13+, other languages, interpreting and non-consecutive days require a separate quote.
