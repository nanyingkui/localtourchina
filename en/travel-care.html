When travel feels uncertain, here is what to check.

From travelling with parents to food, weather and transfers: explore possible adjustments and what needs a separate check. Support scope and contact hours follow your chosen product and booking confirmation.

Parents and walking

What if knees hurt or energy runs low?

Discuss a rest, a shorter return or descent, and a revised pickup point.

Some walking may remain. Extra transport or tickets are checked first.

What if my parents cannot stand in long queues?

Check the queue, reservation constraints and available rest spots before deciding whether to wait or change plans.

Queue-free entry is not guaranteed; any paid priority service is separate.

What if someone is afraid of heights?

Discuss concerns before booking and check which sections can be skipped or replaced.

Some cableways or exposed sections are part of the main access route.

Where are the toilets, steps and rest stops?

Check known facilities before travel; an accompanying guide can confirm the nearest available stop.

This is not a claim that the whole route is accessible without steps.

Can some of us return to the hotel early?

Discuss who accompanies each group, transport and where to reunite.

One guide and one vehicle cannot accompany two groups at once. Extra staff or transport may be needed.

What if the room is noisy or difficult to reach?

Check lifts, beds and room preferences in advance; contact reception if there is a problem.

Room location, changes and upgrades depend on availability and any price difference.

What if someone feels unwell?

Help contact appropriate assistance, explain the location and discuss stopping the itinerary and family arrangements.

Medical assessment belongs to qualified professionals. Accompaniment, translation and transport scope need confirmation.

Food and meal times

What if a dish is still too spicy?

Check chilli and Sichuan pepper separately, explain the cooking request and discuss a replacement if needed.

Available adjustments depend on the restaurant and dish.

What if we dislike certain ingredients?

Collect ingredients to avoid, check them when ordering and discuss familiar alternatives.

Substitutions need the restaurant’s confirmation.

Can meals be softer for older guests?

Ask about soft textures and check suitable dishes and serving times with the restaurant.

The actual menu and portions depend on the restaurant and party.

How are food allergies handled?

Pass the exact ingredients and avoidance needs to the restaurant; ask about ingredients and shared equipment. Discuss an alternative when details cannot be confirmed.

Allergen isolation cannot be guaranteed. Separate preparation and restaurant changes need confirmation.

What if our group has different diets?

Discuss shared dishes and individual portions, including what can be changed within the meal budget.

Strict vegetarian needs include checking stock and sauces. Separate meals may cost extra.

What if we arrive late or miss normal meal times?

Check flights and entry times, then discuss breakfast, a simple meal en route or a later meal.

Each meal’s inclusion must be clear; packed breakfasts require hotel confirmation.

What if we are still hungry or dislike the meal?

Explain the issue, check the remaining meal budget and discuss extra dishes, replacements or the next meal.

Extra orders, drinks, consumed meals and restaurant changes may affect the cost.

Weather, tickets and routes

What if fog hides the scenery?

Compare visibility, forecasts and booking constraints before discussing a different day, visit order or keeping the plan.

Weather cannot be guaranteed. Rebooking and repeat admission may cost extra.

What if rain makes paths slippery?

Check actual operations and the group’s ability, then discuss shorter walks or another activity and return transport.

Confirm what equipment to bring or borrow; free equipment is not assumed.

What if a cableway or attraction closes?

Check the operator’s notice, available routes and alternative visits.

Alternatives may have different prices and require your agreement.

What if tickets or time slots sell out?

Check entrances, routes and bookable times before planning the day; discuss available alternatives.

Assistance does not guarantee tickets, priority access or unlimited changes.

What if a ticket has the wrong route or name?

Check ticket type, identification details and entrance; contact the issuer about corrections.

Tickets bought elsewhere follow the original issuer’s change and refund rules.

Can we add a visit at the last minute?

Check time, tickets, transport and energy, then show the added items and cost before deciding.

A private vehicle is not unlimited use; extra guide hours need confirmation.

Transport and hotels

What if the flight is delayed?

Check flight status, meeting point and contact method, then confirm waiting or a replacement vehicle.

Waiting, night-time and next-day charges follow the transfer terms.

Will our luggage fit?

Check passengers, guide seats and luggage before choosing a vehicle.

Children occupy real capacity. Large luggage, wheelchairs and any upgrade require confirmation.

What if someone gets travel sick?

Discuss the route, feasible stops and rest breaks when booking.

We do not give medication advice; stops depend on road and service conditions.

What if we lose the rest of our group?

Agree a meeting point and contacts beforehand, then check locations and coordinate a reunion.

Separate groups need their own contacts; one guide cannot lead both at once.

How do we get back after Furong’s night view?

Confirm a return service or plan, meeting point, driver contact and departure time.

Late returns and next-day timing matter. Transport outside the booked scope is quoted separately.

What if we risk missing our flight or train?

Work backwards from the carrier’s required arrival time and set a departure time from the last attraction.

Arrival cannot be guaranteed. Delays and ticket changes are handled according to the circumstances and terms.

What if the hotel room differs from the confirmation?

Compare the room with the confirmation and ask reception about repair, a room change or alternatives.

Keep records; availability and price differences need your agreement.

Independent travel

Which park entrance should we use?

Check the hotel, ticket and day’s route, then provide the Chinese place name and access information.

Check current information. Remote support does not include a transfer.

What if the driver cannot find the place?

Use the Chinese name, a location link and an agreed meeting point; check the exact branch when needed.

A translated name alone may not distinguish similarly named hotels or restaurants.

What if payment does not work?

Check the merchant’s accepted methods and discuss contacting the payment provider or a workable alternative.

We do not request payment passwords or promise advances. Charges depend on the provider.

What if my phone has no data or battery?

Prepare meeting details and contacts that can be saved offline, with a backup contact plan.

Online support cannot guarantee contact while you are offline.

Can we leave time to explore on our own?

Separate fixed reservations from free time and identify required and optional activities.

Agree the budget, driver waiting and guide finishing time.

What if belongings or documents are lost?

Check the last known place and contact the relevant attraction or transport provider; clarify the next steps.

Recovery and timing cannot be guaranteed. Continued handling, accompaniment and transport need confirmation.

Costs and responsibilities

How can we avoid unexpected charges?

Check attraction transport, meals, rooms and optional activities separately; confirm the amount before changes.

The payable total and its conditions should be clear, including the actual party size.

What if one traveller cancels?

Recalculate refundable items, rooms and transport and provide a revised quote.

Refunds depend on supplier rules, confirmed terms and costs already incurred.

Can we ask for help after the guide finishes?

Check the urgency, booked hours and available contact options; explain if extra staff are needed.

A day guide, a full itinerary and online support have different service hours.

Does the online support fee cover everything?

Use it for the stated route advice, addresses and information. Purchases require a separate confirmation process.

It does not include in-person accompaniment, unlimited handling, advances or unlimited service.

Who should we contact when something happens?

Confirm the main contact and what the supplier, guide and ticket issuer each handle.

Contact hours and backup arrangements must be stated in the booking confirmation.

What happens after we report a problem?

Record the issue, confirmed service and your request, then discuss the responsible contact and next update.

Response timing and any remedy depend on the case, facts and agreed terms.

How we approach travel

Your trip. Your choices.

We guide carefully, explain first and decide together.

  1. No pressure to buy or add activities

    Shopping and extra visits are optional. Declining a purchase or an extra does not change the service we have agreed to provide.

  2. Extra costs, explained before you choose

    For an additional stop, more guide time or another change, we check what is possible and the cost before you decide.

  3. Walking and food needs come first

    Tell us about stairs, rest breaks and ingredients you find difficult. We discuss practical alternatives within the agreed itinerary.

  4. A concern? Tell the operator

    If the guiding or attitude makes you uncomfortable, contact the LocalTourChina operator. We will understand the situation and discuss workable next steps.

Weather, attraction operations and booking changes remain subject to actual conditions and the service provider’s terms.
