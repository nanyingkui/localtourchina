Day 2

The peaks you looked up at
come to eye level today.

Ten-Mile Gallery → Bailong Elevator up → Yuanjiajie → Tianzi cable car down

Morning · Starting below the mountains

First look up. Then go up.

From your seat on the Ten-Mile Gallery train, look for shapes in the rocks. A shuttle takes you to Bailong Elevator, which rises along the cliff. From the viewpoints, the mountains you saw below appear at a different height.

Today,
the way up
and the way down
are different too.

Up by Bailong Elevator,
down by Tianzi Mountain cable car.
Save your energy
for the viewpoints.

Midday · Yuanjiajie and the First Bridge Under Heaven

Take a photo,
then take in what is outside the frame.

Start with one pillar nearby, then let your gaze follow the mountains layered behind it. Hand your phone to the guide for a photo together. Ask for a wide landscape or a closer picture of your group.

No need to descend just for lunch

After exploring around the First Bridge Under Heaven, have lunch and a seated rest on the mountain. Then continue to the Tianzi viewpoints and the descent.

Afternoon · Down from Tianzi Mountain

This time, enjoy the view sitting down.

After the viewpoints, take your seat in the cable car. Watch the rocks recede and the forest draw closer. Over dinner, find your favourite moments of the day in each other's photos.

Meals & stayBreakfast, lunch and dinner included

Day 3

Trace the mountain ridges,
then walk beside the water below.

Huangshizhai cable car both ways → lunch and rest → Golden Whip Stream

Morning · Huangshizhai

From a single pillar
to mountains layered into the distance.

Enter through the South Gate and take the cable car. At Huangshizhai, follow the mountain outlines beyond the nearby trees. After the viewpoints, take the cable car down for lunch and a rest.

Afternoon · Golden Whip Stream

Alongside this photo,
save the sound of the water.

Agree on the endpoint and return route with your guide first. Pause beside the water and listen. If you like, record just 10 seconds on your phone. A sound from the forest to keep beside this morning's mountain photos.

The second walk follows your group's pace

Today includes walking at both Huangshizhai and Golden Whip Stream. At the entrance, we review your energy and the time left before choosing the section. Completing a fixed long walk is not required.

Meals & stayBreakfast, lunch and dinner included
Stay in the same downtown hotel

Walking & transportWalking at Huangshizhai viewpoints and Golden Whip Stream. Cable car both ways included. Distance and rest stops are planned for your group.

Day 4

Start with the fun of choosing,
then onto Tianmen Mountain.

Town supermarket and coffee → lunch → Tianmen Mountain → dinner and massage

Morning · Town supermarket

One familiar flavour, one new discovery.

Show your guide a packet you are curious about. Check the sweetness, spice and storage details, then choose snacks to share. Purchases are optional and cost extra. The photo shows Jinlimen products; the actual shop follows your route.

One coffee per person included

Take a seat,
and share
yesterday's photos.

Hot or iced? Choose from the available menu and take a break. After an unhurried morning and lunch, head to Tianmen Mountain.

A change of pace before the mountain

After two days in the park, spend a morning in town. Tianmen Mountain's ticket time determines the schedule, with shops and coffee planned around it.

Afternoon · Tianmen Mountain summit

This is what it looks like
to walk into the view.

Follow the railing and stop where the view opens up. Alternate between watching the path ahead and pausing to look out. Tell us before booking if heights worry you so we can review the tickets and possible routes.

Do we have to climb all those steps?

We check transport and escalator routes to reduce the need to climb the 999 steps. The order of ascent and descent depends on your booked ticket and facility operations.

After the mountain: dinner and 80 minutes to unwind

An 80-minute foot and body massage is included. If queues at Tianmen Mountain run long, we arrange to move it to another day, aiming to preserve the full massage time despite travel delays.

Meals & stayBreakfast, lunch and dinner included
Stay in the same downtown hotel

Walking & transportWalking and stairs on Tianmen Mountain. One coffee and an 80-minute massage included. Tell us before booking about concerns with heights or glass floors.

Day 5

Inside the mountain. Across the bridge.
A different scene to the very end.

Check out → Huanglong Cave → lunch → Grand Canyon → dinner → airport

Morning · Huanglong Cave

Yesterday, outside the mountain.
Today, inside it.

After check-out, leave your luggage in the vehicle and carry only a day bag. Look closely at the lit rock textures, then follow the formations up towards the ceiling. Take the walkways and stairs with your guide at your group's pace.

Afternoon · Grand Canyon Glass Bridge

The bridge in the picture
is now beneath our feet.

Check the crossing arrangements at the entrance, then set off together. Look through the glass or towards the mountains ahead—whichever feels comfortable. Take photos without blocking the path. If heights worry you, check possible route adjustments before booking.

After the canyon · Dinner and flight home

One last scene,
close to the water.

Explore the lower canyon according to your booked route. Remember its scale from above and the colours beside the water. After dinner, go straight to the airport, without a return to the hotel for luggage.

The final day is planned backwards from your flight

We set the airport arrival time first, allowing for the journey out of the canyon and dinner. If your flight departs earlier, we revise this day's plan.

Unload the suitcases.
One last photo together.

After the final meal, head to the airport. Before leaving the vehicle, check passports, day bags and anything left on the seats. If time allows, take a last photo with the guide who shared your trip.

Meals & stayBreakfast, lunch and dinner included
To the airport after dinner

Walking & transportStairs in Huanglong Cave and walking in the Grand Canyon. Glass Bridge included. Planned around a late departure flight.

Will it suit our group?

Is it suitable for travelling with parents?

Tell us how much you usually walk and whether stairs are difficult, rather than judging by age alone. Viewpoints, caves and the canyon involve walking. Huangshizhai uses cable cars both ways; we plan Golden Whip Stream and rest stops with you.

I am afraid of glass bridges or heights.

Tell us before booking. We check tickets and entry routes for Tianmen Mountain and the Grand Canyon, then explain possible adjustments. We do not assume you can simply wait alone while the rest of the group continues.

What if it is not clear like the photos?

Fog and rain change the views. We check whether the order can be changed within booked tickets and current operations, explaining the changes and any extra cost first. Clear views or a return visit are not guaranteed.

A good trip starts with clear expectations.

We confirm walking, rest points, hotel rooms and inclusions for your group. Additional visits or more service time are discussed with the cost before you choose.
